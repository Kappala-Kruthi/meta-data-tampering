Forensic Principles: Interpreting Cryptographic Hashes

• Cryptographic Collision Resistance: SHA-256 produces a unique 256-bit (64 hex characters) digest. The chance of two distinct files yielding the exact same hash is astronomically small (less than 1 in 10^77).

• Bit-for-Bit Certainty: If two files have matching SHA-256 hashes, every single bit and byte is identical. They are verified byte duplicates.

• Critical Forensic Limitation: Differing hashes simply indicate that the binary byte sequences are not identical. Re-compression, metadata stripping, format conversion, or pixel editing will all change the hash. A differing hash does NOT by itself prove malicious manipulation.
